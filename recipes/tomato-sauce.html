Recipe for Tomato Sauce

Description

This delicious tomato basil sauce is made by slowly simmering ripe tomatoes, garlic, and fresh basil until rich and sweet for a late summer delight!

Ingredients

  • 8 pounds ripe tomatoes, seeded and diced
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh basil
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 1 large onion, minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook tomatoes and basil in a large saucepan over medium-low heat until tomatoes are soft.
  3. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a medium skillet over medium heat; add onion and garlic and sauté until onion has softened and turned translucent, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add onion mixture to tomato mixture; season with salt and pepper. Simmer on low heat for 2 hours or until thick.
